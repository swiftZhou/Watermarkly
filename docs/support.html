Quick answers

How do I add a watermark?

Open Watermarkly, select photos, choose a mode (Tiled, Corner, Card, or Retouch), adjust the controls, then tap Save All.

Why does Watermarkly need photo access?

Photo access is used so you can choose images to edit and save finished photos to your library. Editing happens on your device.

How do free saves work?

Free users can try a limited number of saves and photo selections. After that, you can unlock unlimited use with a one-time purchase.

How do I restore a purchase?

On the home screen, tap Unlock, then choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you used to buy the unlock.

The logo looks wrong / upside down

Please update to the latest version of Watermarkly, re-select the logo, and try again. If the issue continues, email us with your iOS version and a short description.
